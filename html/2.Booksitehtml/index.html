Why need books in life ?

Books are vital because they provide knowledge and information across diverse subjects, foster imagination and creativity, improve cognitive functions like memory and concentration, build empathy by exposing readers to different perspectives, offer stress relief and entertainment, and promote personal growth by teaching life lessons and new skills. Reading serves as a form of stress relief, can lower heart rate and blood pressure, and is a healthy escape from daily worries.They expose you to different cultures, ideas, and experiences, fostering empathy and a more profound understanding of others' feelings. Life Lessons Books provide insights into managing crises, understanding emotions, and navigating life's challenges, acting as a rich source of wisdom. Reading keeps your brain active, improving brain connectivity and potentially slowing cognitive decline associated with aging.
